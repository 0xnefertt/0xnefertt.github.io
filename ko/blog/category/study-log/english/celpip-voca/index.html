단어뜻예문
relieve안도하게 하다, 완화하다I was relieved to hear that you are okay.
relief안도, 안심It was a relief to hear the good news.
It was good to learn that~라는 것을 알게 되어 좋았다/다행이었다It was good to learn that you are both okay.
It was unfortunate to hear that~라는 것을 듣게 되어 안타까웠다/유감이었다It was unfortunate to hear that your house was damaged.
fortunate운이 좋은, 다행인We were fortunate that nobody was hurt.
tragic비참한, 비극적인It was a tragic accident.
belonging소지품Many people lost their belongings in the flood.
involve관련시키다, 포함하다The recovery work involved many volunteers.
shortage부족There was a shortage of clean water after the storm.
evolve진화하다, 발전하다The situation evolved quickly.
hesitate망설이다Please do not hesitate to ask for help.
tremendous엄청난, 막대한, 대단한The community showed tremendous support.
despite~에도 불구하고Despite the damage, everyone stayed calm.
tackle해결하려고 다루다We need all the help we can get tackling this mess.
