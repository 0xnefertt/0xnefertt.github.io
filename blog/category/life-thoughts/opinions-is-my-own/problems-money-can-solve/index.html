[Notice] Problems Money Can Solve Are the Easiest Problems

0xnefertt2026.09.15.

Can money really not buy happiness??

Of course, everyone’s standard for happiness is different, so perhaps that’s possible.

I used to believe money couldn’t buy happiness.

Looking back now, I think I was young, or full of romantic ideals.

Lately, my thinking has been changing little by little.

I think there is almost nothing in this world that money can’t do.

Maybe life has worn me down and left me a little jaded.

I don’t want to deny it.

“Problems money can solve are the easiest problems.”

“If money doesn’t solve it, consider whether you simply don’t have enough.”

“The claim that money can’t buy happiness is a lie meant to keep the poor from rioting.”

I wish I had a truly enormous amount of money.
