Cookies, analytics, and ads

This site saves your theme preference in your browser. When enabled, Google Analytics measures page visits and interactions, including browser, device, and approximate location information. Google AdSense and other advertising providers may use cookies to show ads based on visits to this and other websites. See Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising partners for details.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser, opt out of personalized ads through Google Ads Settings or AboutAds, and use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Where a consent message is shown, you can manage your choices there.

External services

GitHub Pages hosts the blog. GitHub/Giscus handles comments when enabled, and Open-Meteo and Frankfurter supply weather and exchange rates. Your browser may send these services technical information, such as your IP address, when making requests. Their own privacy policies apply. The owner's writing area uses GitHub sign-in and Cloudflare to store private drafts and login sessions; drafts become public when published.
