| 단어                            | 뜻                                         | 예문                                                    |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| relieve                         | 안도하게 하다, 완화하다                    | I was relieved to hear that you are okay.               |
| relief                          | 안도, 안심                                 | It was a relief to hear the good news.                  |
| It was good to learn that       | ~라는 것을 알게 되어 좋았다/다행이었다     | It was good to learn that you are both okay.            |
| It was unfortunate to hear that | ~라는 것을 듣게 되어 안타까웠다/유감이었다 | It was unfortunate to hear that your house was damaged. |
| fortunate                       | 운이 좋은, 다행인                          | We were fortunate that nobody was hurt.                 |
| tragic                          | 비참한, 비극적인                           | It was a tragic accident.                               |
| belonging                       | 소지품                                     | Many people lost their belongings in the flood.         |
| involve                         | 관련시키다, 포함하다                       | The recovery work involved many volunteers.             |
| shortage                        | 부족                                       | There was a shortage of clean water after the storm.    |
| evolve                          | 진화하다, 발전하다                         | The situation evolved quickly.                          |
| hesitate                        | 망설이다                                   | Please do not hesitate to ask for help.                 |
| tremendous                      | 엄청난, 막대한, 대단한                     | The community showed tremendous support.                |
| despite                         | ~에도 불구하고                             | Despite the damage, everyone stayed calm.               |
| tackle                          | 해결하려고 다루다                          | We need all the help we can get tackling this mess.     |
