(어떻게 업계에 발을 들여놓게 되셨나요?)

What's the most exciting project you're working on right now?
(지금 가장 흥미로운 프로젝트는 무엇인가요?)

Have you attend this event before?
(이전에 이 행사 참석하신 적 있나요?)

(직업에서 가장 즐거운 점은 무엇인가요?)

(네트워킹 이벤트에서 가장 좋아하는 부분은 무엇인가요?)

Have you read any good books or articles lately?
(최근에 좋은 책이나 기사를 읽은 적이 있나요?)

What's the best piece of advice you've ever received?
(지금까지 받은 조언 중 가장 좋았던 것은 무엇인가요?)

Are you working on any personal projects outside of work?
(업무 외에 개인적인 프로젝트를 진행하고 있나요?)

What's your favorite way to unwind after a busy day?
(바쁜 하루를 보낸 후 긴장을 푸는 가장 좋은 방법은 무엇인가요?)

I'm still early in my career.

I'm trying to get into software development.

I'm trying to meet more people in the local tech community.

## 알아두면 좋을 회화형 동사

- figure out (알아내다, 방향을 잡다, 해결하다)

- get into (어떤 분야에 들어가다, 관심을 갖게 되다)

- reach out (연락하다, 먼저 컨택하다)

- follow up (후속 연락하다, 이어서 확인하다)

- work on (~을 하고 있다, ~을 개선하다)

- look for (~을 찾다)

  Yes, I'm currently looking for a new job.

  I'd been looking for junior developer opportunities lately.

- find out (알게 되다, 알아내다)

- break into (업계에 진입하다)

  I'd like to break into blockchain industry in Canada.

  I'm trying to break into

- be interested in (~에 관심이 있다)

- make sense (말이 되다, 이해되다, 합리적이다)

- end up (결국 ~하게 되다)

- pick up (배우다, 익히다)

- get a sense of (감을 잡다, 분위기를 파악하다)

  Yes, I'm getting a sense of tech industry.

- learn from (~에게서 배우다)

- be open to (~에 열려있다)

- be currently focused on (현재 ~에 집중하고 있다)

- from what I've heard (제가 듣기로는)

  From what I've heard, Canada care a lot about communication and culture fit, not just technical skills, so I'm trying to improve both.

It was really nice talking with you. I'd love to stay in touch and maybe follow up later if I have a few questions. Would it be okay if I connected with you on LinkedIn or text?
