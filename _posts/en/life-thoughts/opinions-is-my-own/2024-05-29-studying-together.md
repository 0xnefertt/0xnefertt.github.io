This isn't a post telling you to study with ChatGPT...

![](/assets/img/posts/c1711522-f4b7-5faf-a8f3-066155dd45bd/ca91f971-ec54-5885-abdc-14effbe277b6.jpg)

When we study or try to learn something, we have all kinds of options: teaching ourselves, forming a study group with like-minded people, getting help through tutoring or classes, or working with ChatGPT. Everyone may have their own preference, but the thing to be careful about is not to take a completely solitary, lone-wolf approach.

![](/assets/img/posts/c1711522-f4b7-5faf-a8f3-066155dd45bd/bf628223-a93e-588c-acc4-9fc0f02137f1.png)

I think **metacognition** is one of the important abilities we need in life these days. When you study alone, you can sometimes convince yourself you know something even when you don't. The same goes for investing: if you catch yourself thinking, "I'm a little special..." or "This time will be different...", take that as a sign that your metacognition is lacking. The more this happens, the more likely you are to be lenient with yourself and strict with others.

To develop metacognition, reverse that way of thinking. Don't forget to be honest with yourself and more understanding of others.

![](/assets/img/posts/c1711522-f4b7-5faf-a8f3-066155dd45bd/506a9c2b-da7d-5d0b-aa8c-85be153b265c.png)

It's true that this is a great time to learn through YouTube and blogs, but there are things to watch out for. You must learn best practices from people with a proven track record. Especially when it comes to investing, it can be difficult to distinguish experts from individual investors. Even if you join a trading-signal group, I recommend at least figuring out whether the person makes money by selling signals or by actually investing. Personally, when it comes to crypto investing, I think anyone who calls themselves an "expert" or "master" is someone you should definitely avoid.

![](/assets/img/posts/c1711522-f4b7-5faf-a8f3-066155dd45bd/b897cd83-ff9d-5ce7-a714-a4db0913dc9d.png)

Never assume you should simply grit your teeth and study alone. In a competitive society, being compared with others and evaluated by someone else isn't unreasonable. Whatever method you use, develop the habit of reading officially released technical documents and posts on official blogs, rather than news floating around the internet. The best approach is to ask someone who is actually earning money in the field.
