Just a year ago,

the phrase "every person for themselves" lodged itself in my mind.

It was when the first digit of my age changed from two to three.

Looking back now, I was reckless and immature then.

It was also a time when I lacked a sense of responsibility.

The same is true even now.

Comparing myself today with who I was a year ago, not much has changed.

But I've experienced so much,

and ahead of me lie only things that will bring more responsibility.

I feel a tangle of emotions that are hard to explain,

but if I were to put it in one sentence,

I think it would be: "My main job comes first."

At least once in life, everyone faces a moment so difficult that they feel like letting go of life itself.

If it's something I have to do anyway, pain is only natural.

If I can get it over with, it's better to do so quickly.
