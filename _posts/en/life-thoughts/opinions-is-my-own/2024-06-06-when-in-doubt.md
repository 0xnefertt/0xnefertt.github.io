There's a saying that "life is a series of choices and decisions." And everyone has probably found themselves wondering, "**Should I do it or not?**" at least once. I wrote this post to record my thoughts on ways to make that decision clearly and quickly.

1\. When you're wondering, "Should I do it or not?", just do it.

2\. If you're thinking, "Is it okay to do this?", don't do it.

3\. If you're thinking, "Is it okay to skip this?", you should do it.

4\. It's better to try something and regret it than to regret never trying.

5\. For example, even if you wonder, "It's so hot today—should I go to the gym or not?", going will leave you feeling good and proud of yourself. Another example: during exam season, you might think, "I really want to spend today having fun. Is it okay not to study?" You've probably ended up regretting that afterward, thinking, "I should have studied then..."

6\. If you look closely at the meaning of "life is a series of choices and decisions," those choices include our thoughts and actions. Those thoughts and actions produce further thoughts that shape who we are. We grow by going through more of this process of becoming ourselves, so regardless of the outcome, when an idea comes to mind, putting it into action is the right thing to do.

7\. I don't know how you found this blog post, but if you have something in mind, I encourage you to go ahead and put it into practice.
