As everyone knows, humans are born with the distinctive ability to **think**. We describe it as a key characteristic that distinguishes us from animals, and say we differ from them in many ways, including logical reasoning, emotion, and creativity.

It has been quite a while since I last wrote a post. In the meantime, I spent a lot of time wondering what topics I should write about on my blog.

Many people on YouTube recommend consistently posting about a single topic when starting a blog. The reason is the idea of a “money-making blog.”

Most people tend to start blogging with the thought, “If I am writing anyway, wouldn't it be nice if my blog could also earn some money?”

You have probably seen someone on social media say there has never been an easier time to make money. Even self-help books make becoming wealthy sound incredibly easy.

Perhaps because we are exposed to so much of this kind of content, it can be difficult to tell what is real and what is not. Sometimes, though, it all sounds painfully predictable.

“Should I quit my job and try YouTube?”

“Should I write blog posts after work and earn some pocket money?”

“I like food, so should I visit restaurants and become an influencer?”

Anyone can easily have these thoughts,

and say these things so casually.

Humans are thinking beings. By making the most of this ability, we have built a better world and evolved to meet the future. Of course, misguided thoughts lead to misguided actions. But without thinking, we would not even know whether an action was wrong.

Thinking is very important. But we should not stop at thinking; we need to act. Thoughts that never become action are no better than crap. Fortunately, thoughts are fleeting, so it is good to save them somewhere just as they are. Keeping personal notes is a good habit, but from now on, I intend to write my thoughts on this blog.

Humans are beings capable of putting what they think into action.
