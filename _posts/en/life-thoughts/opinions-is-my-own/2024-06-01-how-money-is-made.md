While looking for a topic for today's blog post, I happened to notice an article about venture capital.

1\. VC means venture capital, or a venture capital investment manager or investor. In other words, VCs mainly discover and invest in early-stage startups or companies, help them grow, and earn a greater return. It struck me as quite similar to crypto.

2\. Then what do VCs look at closely when they invest? There may be many factors, but ultimately it must be whether the company is capable of making money for them.

![](/assets/img/posts/bc37d30f-322e-5ea5-ac98-a37e972ad730/a9a49caf-bbc4-5243-a52d-abd3bb39e4b6.png)

3\. THE VC is a website where you can track various VCs and investment trends among Korean startups. I recognized a few companies on the list. I'd assumed they were making enough money not to need investment, but apparently not.

4\. I've started to believe that studying how money is made could be useful in life. Of course, I don't have any immediate desire or plan to start a business, but I expect I'll continue investing throughout my life.

5\. I'll keep building a blog where I record my thoughts from this perspective, and through it, try to find an answer to the question: what position can I occupy?

6\. Whatever position you're in, money has the power to move people. That's why we need to understand how money flows.

7\. Let's think about how the companies closest to us, or the services we're using right now, make their money.
