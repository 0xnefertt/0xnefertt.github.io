I always emphasize the importance of metacognition in life and try to look at myself objectively, but lately that hasn't been going well. I don't know exactly why, but I can feel the gaps between people widening, and problems that once seemed vague are becoming real.

With the arrival of the AI era, I spend a lot of time wondering what I should do next. AI will keep advancing, and I'm afraid that if something I start is eventually replaced by AI, all the time and effort I put into it might feel pointless. I actively use AI myself, so I'm grateful to be living in this era. Yet there is also a vague fear. I agree with the many articles and arguments that AI could threaten humanity, but I also agree with those who say it will benefit the world. Perhaps that looks contradictory.

Even outside AI, I seem to hold a lot of contradictory thoughts and attitudes. My desire to avoid conflict with people whenever possible may be one reason. People who are overly positive also put me off. On the other hand, I don't want to deal with overly negative people either. Maybe I'm tired of the world's extreme tendency to split into opposing camps, but I've been living with a simple '**let's keep things agreeable**' attitude. You can see it in the way I tell people not to invest in stocks or crypto, while continuing to invest myself. I'm optimistic about crypto's future. But at the same time, I'm pessimistic.

Perhaps I'm closer to an optimistic pessimist than either an optimist or a pessimist.

> _"Hope for the best, prepare for the worst"_

Expect the best, but be ready for the worst.

I don't know how the future will change. I just want to be someone who is optimistic without letting their guard down, and pessimistic without giving up.
