Everyone is given the same 24 hours. It may sound clichéd, but while life isn't fair, time is. And it is true that time, once gone, cannot be recovered. In that sense, who I was yesterday creates who I am today. My past actions and thoughts have come together to make me who I am now, and what I am doing now will shape my future self. Unless the world stops, time keeps moving, so we need to recognize that this very moment is both the youngest we will ever be and something precious. That is why we should value every moment and live with effort.

Many people already know that they shouldn't waste time. But I often fail to appreciate it because I think of it as a gift I received for free, with an unlimited supply still to come. In particular, among the things we do on our phones, social media and watching YouTube seem to be common ways of letting time slip away.

There are many ways to use time efficiently. If you have plenty of money, you can buy someone else's time. Paying for knowledge that someone spent time acquiring is also a way of buying the time they invested. One of the most cost-effective ways to use your time well is to enter their league. In other words, you need to change your surroundings. If you're going to spend the same eight to ten hours out of your day anyway, it's much better to spend them with smarter people. By entering their league, you can gain many experiences and learn new things. Maybe that's why people try so hard to get into good universities and climb higher on the social ladder.

Let's always hold firmly to the determination to be the best.
