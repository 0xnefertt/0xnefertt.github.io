You have probably seen book titles such as “Laws for Avoiding Losses in the Stock Market” or “Laws for Surviving the Real Estate Market” in a bookstore.

![Related image](/assets/img/posts/2023-08-12.png)

The image above shows the definition of “law” in Naver's Korean dictionary.

A “law” is something **absolute**.

What we call a “law” needs to be interpreted and applied differently

across fields and situations.

Of course, a **law** can be broken.

But it does not change easily.

**Laws** are built on experience.

That is why drawing on the situations in which a law arose

can help us make decisions.

If we know the definitions and backgrounds of various laws,

a day will come, at least once in our lives,

when that knowledge proves useful.

**Laws** are products of the experiences people have accumulated over a long time.

Then,

do the **laws of success** that so many people seek really exist...?

If you search YouTube,

you will find various quotations from successful people

and hear about the lives they have led.

If you apply those lessons directly to your own life and follow them,

will you become successful?

No.

The experiences and perspectives of successful people are certainly important and can offer valuable insights.

However, your current circumstances and the backgrounds of those successful people will be entirely different.

That is why there is no universal

**“law of success” that applies equally to everyone.**

Nevertheless,

if we want to succeed,

we must keep searching without losing heart.

By continually exploring within our own circumstances and making the right choices,

we must strive to lead meaningful, fulfilling lives.

That is the life I will work toward.
