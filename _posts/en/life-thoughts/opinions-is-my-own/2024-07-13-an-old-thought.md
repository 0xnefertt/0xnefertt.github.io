Lately, I've often felt that society is becoming sick.

Whichever online community, news outlet, or YouTube channel I visit, I frequently see people fighting.

Common topics include real estate, politics, the birth rate, gender conflict, and employment, along with claims that Korea will soon collapse.

Just a few years ago, livestreamers were making provocative content and tearing each other down for attention. Now that has become a whole genre known as "rekka"—online drama chasers.

On AfreecaTV, viewers who excessively adore and defend female streamers are called **"water buffaloes."** (Learning why might give you the chills.)

Another expression used to describe Koreans is **"pot mentality"**—heating up quickly and cooling down just as fast.

I think those two tendencies, together with Instagram and its **culture of showing off**, are damaging much of Korean culture today.

Maybe people have been trapped in the online world since COVID-19... or maybe they've been steeped in dopamine. Either way, people seem angrier.

And there's far too much interference in other people's affairs.

Judging by the stories that have been appearing in the news lately, gender conflict and birth-rate issues will intensify, and conflicts between older and younger people will emerge as well.

People of every age and gender divide each other into camps, flock to one side, then get swept over to the other. That's the reality of our country today.

As men and women, young and old, all find themselves opposed to one another, Korea inevitably becomes more individualistic.

We tend to think of neighboring Japan—the country we so often dislike and treat as an adversary—as highly individualistic. But lately, Korea has become just as individualistic, and increasingly materialistic as well.

After thinking carefully about the mindset that might help me survive this chaos, I think there are two things worth keeping in mind.

1\. Take an impossible-to-argue-with attitude: "Yes, it's because I'm stupid. It's because I didn't know any better. It's because I'm too nice."

2\. Don't give power to a jerk. In other words, don't become one yourself. Don't act like a big shot.
