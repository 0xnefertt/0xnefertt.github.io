These seem to have been the most talked-about celebrities around me lately.

The title says "the rich," but Han Ye-seul and IU are really in the stratosphere of the entertainment industry. And yes, they're probably rich, too...

Actress Han Ye-seul's social media posts revealing her boyfriend, and the Lamborghini she gave him...

And then IU buying a 13-billion-won apartment outright, in a single cash payment...

Honestly, I wasn't usually interested, but people around me kept talking about it so much that I ended up finding out anyway.

Celebrities make a living from the attention and affection of the public,

but these are personal matters. Do they really need to become news stories?

Personally, when I see articles like that, I think "Wow... okay..." is about the right reaction.

Something you can mention casually at a café or over drinks to fill an awkward silence? Isn't anything beyond that crossing a line?

And there's no need to go out of your way to criticize them or leave nasty comments.

It just looks like an unnecessary inferiority complex, and they won't care anyway.

You don't need to worry about rich people, whether they're celebrities or not.

Never. Absolutely never.

Worry about yourself.
