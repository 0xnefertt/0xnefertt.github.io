It feels as though we were saying "Happy New Year" just the other day. I can't believe it's already May.

From January through March, I more or less muddled along.

Actually, I had a trip to Korea planned for April, and the anticipation kept me going.

Going back to Korea was the biggest event for me.

I spent the month of April there.

Visiting Korea brought up feelings I'd never experienced while living there. I don't know whether I've written this before, but I dislike the phrase "the smarter you are, the sooner you leave Korea." No matter how severe the divisions between genders and generations or the political crises, Korea is still undoubtedly a good country to live in. I probably wouldn't have realized that if I'd continued living there.

And strangely, some Koreans living in Canada seem to hate Korea intensely. I can't understand it, and if I can avoid people like that, I'd like to.

One thing that always comes up when discussing Korea is its culture of constantly comparing people. In the past, I probably would have thought that tendency was unhealthy too, but now I wonder whether it's really bad—as long as you're not putting others down through those comparisons. While working in Korea, I may not have noticed because I was surrounded by that culture. Of course, it was stressful, and pursuing a higher salary meant I felt compelled to live an intensely disciplined, productive life. At the same time, I came to think that living diligently isn't necessarily a bad thing.

I think unfairness would be a more reasonable reason to dislike Korea than comparison itself. In online communities, I sometimes see demoralizing comments like "They're kicking away the ladder upward" or "No more dragons will rise from humble streams." But there are circumstances we cannot choose from birth, so everyone inevitably starts somewhere different. I think we have to accept that to some extent, and beyond that, I still feel there are opportunities. Perhaps living abroad is what helped me realize this, too.

I reflect on how shameful it was to speak badly of my own country. Especially now, when its standing is rising, I shouldn't go against that.

Separately, I should become more active on the blog, if only to leave a record.

It's a promise I fail to keep every time, but...

I'm making the commitment again.
