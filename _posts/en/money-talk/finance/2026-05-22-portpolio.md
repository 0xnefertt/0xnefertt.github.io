### Reasons for the Allocation

1. I feel intense FOMO about Korean stocks, but I am too afraid of rushing in to buy.

2. At the time, Warren Buffett said to keep cash on hand, so I began gradually buying the safe-haven assets gold and silver.

3. Fuel and minerals are inseparable from Canadian industry, so I added ENB and XGD, which seemed dependable and attractive for dividends.

4. I had held Nike for a while, but I never expected it to fall this much.

### Looking Back

1. Excluding Shopify and the Big Five banks, ENB is effectively Canada's largest company by market capitalization. A large-cap stock in a country's core industry is indeed a reasonable choice as a form of insurance.

2. Let me not expect too much from Canada. It seems particularly far behind in advanced industries and new technology.

3. Even the combined market capitalization of Canada's top ten companies does not match Samsung Electronics.

4. Whether in real estate or stocks, if you want to hold just one quality asset, it really needs to be a good one.

5. If you want to invest money and not worry about it, an ETF seems the right choice.
