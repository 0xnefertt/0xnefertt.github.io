As I get older, I increasingly want to do something of my own.

How exciting would it be to build something entirely mine…

Just thinking about it makes my heart race.

The reality, though, probably will not be so easy.

Whenever I feel that way, I should come back and read this…

> For \[a particular group of people\],
>
> I solve \[a particular problem\]
>
> and help them reduce \[money spent/time spent/stress\].

If you can answer this confidently, it might be worth getting started.
