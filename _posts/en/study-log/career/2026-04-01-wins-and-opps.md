I happened to come across the phrase “wins & opps” while reading a fellow blogger’s post.

It was my first time hearing it, so I asked ChatGPT. Apparently, it is shorthand for things that went well (or achievements) and areas for improvement. Put simply, it is a performance report. I am not sure whether my current company will negotiate my salary or simply notify me of an increase, but it seems to be talent-review season. The blogger said they had completed a Talent Review.

It varies by company and role, but the categories were as follows.

What you did - MSO and Job Description

What you did - Business Goalds

How you did it - Competencies

How you can grow - Ongoing Development

How you can grow - Career Aspirations

I remember doing something similar in Korea, but perhaps I have become less alert to these things and settled into this life. It has already been almost a year since I joined the company. I think I have done a lot during that time, but results are what ultimately matter. I should think through the categories above to evaluate myself.

There might be an unexpected opportunity to negotiate my salary… And it would also help me prepare early for a job change. I really want to change jobs this year.
